Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how Qelzavryn handles information when you visit and use this website.

This website is maintained as part of the QELZAVRYN collection. For questions about this policy or the website, email contact.qelzavryn@gmail.com.

When you visit the website, hosting and network providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, requested pages, and access timestamps to deliver the site, maintain security, and diagnose issues. Contacting us

If you send us an email, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your message and manage correspondence. Please do not send passwords, bank details, or other sensitive information by email.

Your choices

You can control cookies and locally stored site data in your browser settings. Clearing site data may also remove saved progress or records. You may contact us to ask about information you have emailed us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the website or the services it uses change. The revised version will be posted on this page.
